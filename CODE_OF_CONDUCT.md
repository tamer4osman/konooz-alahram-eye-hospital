# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project serves patients, families, and healthcare staff in Benghazi. We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone who contributes or participates, regardless of background, language, ability, or identity.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:

- Using respectful language in Arabic or English.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Prioritizing patient safety, accessibility, and truthful messaging over convenience.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discriminatory jokes, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Introducing misleading claims about medical services or data handling.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to [info@konozealahram-eye.ly](mailto:info@konozealahram-eye.ly). All reports are reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributors whose behavior violates this code.
